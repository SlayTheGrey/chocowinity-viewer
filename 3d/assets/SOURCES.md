# layout3d asset library: sources, dimensions, [verify] list

All geometry is procedural three.js, built at N scale (1:160) in inches. 1 in = 25.4 mm = 13.33 prototype ft.
`registry.json` / `registry.js` are generated by `test/gen_registry.js`, which loads `gallery.html` headless and **measures** every builder (130 assets).

The plans give track as polylines, not as piece lists, so the viewer **generates Unitrack along the path** with the same profile. Turnouts are placed as real #6/#4 assets at their points. Individual pieces are in the registry and gallery.

## Kits (assets/structures.js)
Footprints come from the plan documents and the Walthers/DPM/WS published dimensions. Every height marked `verify` is an estimate:
Merchant's Row I/II/III (933-3850/3224/3851, heights), 933-3856 (kit number), 933-3233/3855, 933-3854 (name), 933-3861/3862/3864 (names; plan kitbash names), 933-3265, 933-3812 (water tower kit no.), 933-3268, DPM 50200, Iowa Scaled Engineering bin part no., Pamlico Polymers kitbash (estimated).
The viewer fits each kit to the plan footprint, so plan footprints win over catalogue sizes.

## Scenery, vehicles, people, rolling stock
Sizes are prototype dimensions ÷ 160: sedan 15.5 ft, pickup 19 ft, semi 22 ft tractor + 53 ft trailer, school bus 40 ft, people 5'9", loblolly pine 55–80 ft, hardwoods 40–60 ft, utility pole 35 ft, streetlight 25 ft.
Cars: 50 ft boxcar, 4750 cu ft covered hopper, 30k gal tank, 53 ft well, 89 ft autorack, 85 ft Amfleet; locos from EMD/GE published lengths.
Liveries are drawn at runtime on a canvas atlas (roadnames and reporting marks are representative, not specific prototype cars).
