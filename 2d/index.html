How to play

Goal: work your job's switch list: set out every set out car on its industry track and bring every pick up car back into your train, without violations. Through trains are run by the AI dispatcher (Amtrak > intermodal > manifest > locals) and use the elevator lift. They run by train number, as on the prototype: odd numbers counter-clockwise, even numbers clockwise; the dispatcher keeps opposing trains off the same level.

Next move (top of the side panel) tells you what to do next for your job; the target track glows yellow on the map and the turnouts to line are ringed (green = set). Click a car card to focus on that car.

  • Throttle (right): mouse wheel/drag on the knob or ↑/↓; R reverses (stopped only); Space = step 0; E = emergency stop; 0–8 = F0–F8 (F1 bell, F2 horn crossing pattern, F3 short horn). CV3/CV4 = momentum. 28/128 steps (click the step label). +MU adds a loco to the consist.
  • Turnouts: click the dot at the points (green = normal, orange = reverse). Throwing under a car derails it; running through one set against you is a run-through. #4 turnouts: ≤ 10 smph diverging and no cars over 68 ft (reefers, centerbeams, 89 ft flats, autoracks, Amfleet).
  • Uncoupling is by hand: stop, then click the white coupler dot between two cars. Coupling is automatic on contact at ≤ 4 smph (4–8 = hard coupling violation, more = collision).
  • Lift: 18 in in 8 s; it only moves with every car on it wholly aboard and stopped (interlock). Driving into the pit derails.
  • Speed limits: main 40, sidings 15, spurs/yard 10 smph. Blue-flagged tracks (blue) must not be entered.
  • View: wheel = zoom, drag = pan, double-click = reset. Ride along follows the selected loco (cab-style, track ahead up). Follow: click a person or vehicle.
  • Modes: Mixed (you run your job), Automated (AI crews run the planned moves — watch or ride), Manual CTC (you line turnouts and the lift for all trains; the AI only drives).
  • Signals / CTC: block signals sit at each end of every main and siding (green clear, yellow approach, red stop). Click one (map or CTC panel, key C) to hold it at stop; AI trains obey. The CTC panel lines whole routes. The dispatcher breaks head-on standoffs by backing the lower-priority train clear for a meet. Y = yardmaster screen, V = speak radio chatter (Web Speech).
  • P = pause, H = help, M = mute. Save/Load keep one session in this browser.

Data: track graph, industries and move plans from build_ops.py (layout.json + ops.json + roster.json). Scale speed in/s = mph × 17.6 / 160.
